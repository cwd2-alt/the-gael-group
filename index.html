Why The Gael Group

A different kind of
consulting partner.

We bring together motivated Saint Mary’s students, structured consulting methods, and a practical approach to problem-solving. Our teams are built to dig into a question, understand the business, and deliver work that gives clients a clearer path forward.

For organizations, that means an outside perspective without the overhead of a traditional consulting engagement. For our students, it means learning how real businesses work.
